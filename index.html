ONLINE TUITION:

We tend to forget what is actual teaching. The basic word "teach" means "to impart knowledge, give instruction, or help someone acquire a skill or experience". When it comes to Online teaching for Maths and Physics, the most difficult process is when the teacher or student needs to write during the teaching or learning session. So certain type of "mechanism" needs to be present during the live session which can be divided into "software" or "manual writing". Believe me "softwares" are not cheap and normally available softwares like word, excel, paint, etc. do not give you the freedom of accessibilty to writing this symbols. We come to "manual writing", which means the teacher and student writes on a piece of paper and the other party can view directly on line without any kind of delay. So, there has to be a camera setup by both parties, which includes a webcam + table camera holder installed directly on the table and has to have a view of whatever which is written on the paper.
